01

Privacy policy

How we handle information connected to your CHAT TRIPLE A account and activity.

The details

We collect what helps people connect, and keep it protected.

A

Account and profile information

When you sign up, we may collect your name, phone number or email address, date of birth, profile photo, username, and the details you choose to add to your profile.

B

Messages, calls, and posts

We process messages, video and voice calls, comments, photos, videos, and other content you create or share so the features work, abuse can be addressed, and your account stays secure. We do not sell your personal information.

C

Groups and marketplace

We use information about group membership, group activity, marketplace listings, messages between buyers and sellers, and transactions or reports to provide these features, prevent fraud, and enforce our rules.

D

Your choices and safety

You can manage profile visibility, block or report people, control notifications, and ask to access, correct, export, or delete your information. Email privacy@chattriplea.example to get started.

Read our full privacy commitments +

We retain personal information only for as long as needed for the purposes described here, unless a longer period is required by law or needed to resolve disputes, enforce our terms, or protect users. We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards to protect it.

Some features, including calls, messaging, groups, and marketplace services, may use carefully selected service providers that process information on our behalf. They may only use that information to provide contracted services and must protect it appropriately.

Please do not share sensitive personal information in public posts, groups, calls, or marketplace listings. Content you share with other users may be copied, saved, or shared by them.

We may update this policy from time to time. When changes are material, we will provide a more prominent notice and update the date at the top of this page.
